Customer Complaint Policy
1. Purpose
This policy defines how customer complaints are recorded, reviewed, and resolved fairly.
2. Service rules
• A complaint must receive a reference number.
• Urgent cases must be reviewed within one business day.
• Customers must receive a written decision.
Priority | Response target
Urgent | 1 business day
Normal | 5 business days
Information request | 7 business days
